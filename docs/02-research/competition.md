# Competition and alternatives

VoltYard's differentiation is a hypothesis: combine a VIN record, manufacturer-specific battery instructions, charging coordination and handover evidence for terminals serving multiple brands.

## Comparison framework

| Alternative | Likely strength | What to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| TOS / yard management, including INFORM | Vehicle inventory, location and movement planning | Existing battery modules, extension APIs and commercial roadmap |
| Fleet/telematics platforms | Connected vehicle observations and fleet charging | Access to unregistered customer-owned vehicles and OEM permissions |
| Charge point management systems | Charger control, metering and session operations | VIN matching, storage inspections and carrier departure rules |
| Damage inspection / handover tools | Images, condition records and claims evidence | Battery observations and integration into handover dossiers |
| Mobile charging providers | Physical energy delivery without fixed infrastructure | Dispatch planning, data export and equipment integration |
| Internal tools and spreadsheets | Familiar workflows, local adaptations and low marginal cost | Auditability, maintenance, fragmented evidence and migration cost |

Public marketing pages do not establish that a competitor lacks a feature. The previous absolute uniqueness claim is withdrawn. Request demonstrations and test the same scenario across products before drawing a conclusion.

## Comparison exercise

Use a fictional manifest containing a low-SOC vehicle, an overdue inspection, a model-specific profile and a departure maximum. Ask each provider to demonstrate profile provenance, inspection history, task assignment, quarantine release controls, API access and report export. Score evidence as demonstrated, documented, claimed or unknown.

## Positioning

A focused EV operations layer can complement an existing TOS. The integration opportunity may be more credible than a replacement sale. Potential partners include yard software vendors, charging providers and damage-inspection platforms. No partnership is currently agreed.

## Decision gate

If existing tools meet the workflow at lower total cost, prefer an integration or change focus. Interview operators about unmet work rather than asking them to endorse the proposed product. Relevant starting links are in [sources](sources.md).

# Market context

## Initial market

Start with automotive terminals and vehicle compounds handling several manufacturers in the Antwerp-Bruges area. Geographic proximity makes observation, interviews and a supervised pilot practical. Expand only after the workflow and buying process are understood.

The relevant unit is not simply annual port throughput. Software workload depends on simultaneously stored EVs, dwell time, inspection frequency, departure changes and the number of distinct manufacturer profiles.

## Workflow hypothesis

Longer dwell creates more opportunities for battery inspections and charging work. Different OEM and carrier instructions create coordination work. VoltYard addresses that coordination and its evidence record; it does not claim terminals currently lack all suitable software.

## Bottom-up sizing model

```text
Annual EV records = terminal vehicle arrivals × EV share
Average active EV inventory ≈ annual EV records × mean dwell days / 365
Annual recurring revenue = contracted site fee + billable EV records × record price
```

Illustrative assumptions, not market facts: 100,000 EV records/year, 20 days average dwell and €0.50 per record imply approximately 5,479 active EVs and €50,000 record revenue before a site fee, discounts, support, taxes and costs. Model sensitivity to EV share and dwell separately.

## What to collect

| Input | Preferred evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Annual arrivals and EV mix | Terminal-provided operational totals | Operations |
| Dwell distribution | Anonymised arrival/departure extract | Planner |
| Inspection and recovery workload | Observed process plus task sample | Field supervisor |
| Existing systems and interfaces | Vendor/system inventory | IT |
| Customer chargeability | Contract review and account interviews | Commercial |

## Research discipline

Earlier concept research included press estimates and planned terminal expansions. Those numbers are not used as verified current facts here. Retain source dates, distinguish site/group/port totals and avoid counting the same vehicle at multiple handling points.

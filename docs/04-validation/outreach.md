# Outreach plan and English message templates

## Target roles

| Organisation category | Examples to investigate | Relevant roles |
|---|---|---|
| Automotive terminals around Zeebrugge | ICO, Wallenius Wilhelmsen, DP World, CLdN | Yard planner, operations supervisor, HSE, PDI/technical services, IT |
| Antwerp terminals and inland compounds | Confirm active sites before contacting | Operations, IT and commercial owners |
| Networks and introductions | APZI, Voka, Alfaport Voka, VIL, Port of Antwerp-Bruges, course staff | Member relations, innovation and placement coordinators |
| Customers and risk stakeholders | Vehicle importers, OEM logistics teams, marine cargo insurers | Logistics manager, claims specialist |

These are research leads, not existing customers or partners. Verify current organisation details and choose relevant public contact channels. Keep personal contact data and responses outside the public repository.

> Hi [first name], I wanted to follow up on my research question. If a conversation is not practical, an introduction to a colleague in planning, technical services or HSE would also help. Thank you for considering it.

## Network introduction request

> I am researching EV handling workflows for a Full Stack Development final project. Could you introduce me to a terminal or PDI team willing to discuss inspections, charging and evidence records for 20 minutes? Findings will be anonymised, and I would be happy to share the project outcome.

## Thank-you

> Thank you for your time today. Your example about [specific point] helped clarify [finding]. I will use it anonymously and would welcome any correction to my understanding. I can share the final demonstration when it is ready.

## Tracking

Maintain organisation, role, channel, invitation date, follow-up date, consent and outcome in a private tracker. Do not invent response rates or claim completed interviews. Review each message before sending; this repository contains templates only.

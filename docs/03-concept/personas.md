# Personas

These are fictional research personas. Validate them through interviews rather than treating their needs as confirmed.

| Persona | Work context | Main question | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maya, yard planner | Desktop, changing vessel and truck schedules | What needs attention before the next departure? | Prioritised alerts, reliable location and readiness checks |
| Yusuf, field operator | Phone, gloves, variable connectivity | What do I inspect and where do I record it? | Large controls, clear VIN, short form and immediate feedback |
| Elise, HSE lead | Desktop and incident response | Which concerns remain unresolved and who decided? | Quarantine inventory, reasoned decisions and emergency export |
| Thomas, account manager | Customer contact and monthly reconciliation | Which sessions and kWh belong to this OEM? | Explainable energy totals and vehicle evidence |
| Administrator | Configuration and access management | Who can change thresholds and records? | Restricted commands and versioned profiles |
| OEM reader, future | External organisation | What happened to my vehicles? | Read-only, customer-scoped access after tenant security exists |

## Roles in the current application

Admin can execute every command. Planner handles arrivals, inspections, movements, departures and charging. Operator records inspections, moves vehicles and starts/stops approved charging. HSE opens concerns, moves vehicles and records quarantine decisions. Account is read-only. Every demo role currently reads the same single terminal.

## Accessibility and field requirements

Keyboard access, labelled controls, readable severity text, responsive tables and 44px or larger common input targets are implementation goals. Camera scanning and offline synchronisation are still backlog items. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate a safety state.

## Questions for research

Can staff use personal phones? Who owns a quarantine release? Which actions need a second approval? What connectivity exists between storage rows? Which information is visible on the vehicle dashboard? What language does the workforce use?

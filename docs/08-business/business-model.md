# Business model hypotheses

## Customer and buyer

Initial user: planning, field and HSE teams at terminals or compounds serving multiple vehicle manufacturers. Potential buyer: operations/terminal manager with IT, HSE and procurement approval. A user's interest does not establish budget ownership.

## Value proposition

Reduce the time spent locating inspection information, prioritising battery work and reconstructing handover evidence. Validate these savings against current tools before assigning a monetary value.

## Lean model

| Element | Hypothesis |
|---|---|
| Problem | Fragmented observations, changing OEM/carrier instructions and manual work coordination |
| Segment | Multi-brand terminals/compounds with prolonged EV storage |
| Solution | VIN history, approved profiles, task visibility, safety workflow and handover |
| Channel | Direct discovery, logistics networks and TOS/charging partners |
| Revenue | Site subscription, usage tier and separately scoped onboarding/integration |
| Cost | Hosting, database, support, security, integration maintenance, training and insurance/legal review |
| Advantage to test | Clear evidence model and repeatable profile/workflow onboarding |
| Key metrics | Activated users, inspection completion, record reconstruction time, retained sites and support burden |

## Pricing experiment

Compare a fixed site fee with a base-plus-usage model. Illustrative discussion points: €250–€1,000/site/month or €0.50–€1.50 per EV record, subject to minimums, volume and support. These are unvalidated hypotheses, not an offer or market benchmark. Avoid charging for every inspection if that discourages accurate recording.

## Pilot proposal

One site, approved profiles, a limited vehicle cohort, named process/data owners and a measured baseline. Define duration, training, support hours, allowed data, success criteria and stop criteria before starting. A pilot is not a safety-system replacement.

## Commercial risks

Procurement may prefer an existing TOS module; integration and training costs may exceed software fees; liability and data access may dominate purchasing. Test willingness to commit time and budget rather than relying on positive interview sentiment.

## Next decision

Do not estimate a credible addressable market by multiplying unverified port volumes by a price. Build a named-site pipeline, validate arrivals/dwell/workload and measure acquisition/support cost. See [market context](../02-research/market-context.md).

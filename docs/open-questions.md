# Open questions

| Question | Owner / evidence needed | Release impact |
|---|---|---|
| Which school assessment requirements are mandatory? | Supervisor and rubric | Submission scope |
| Is the workflow painful enough to replace/augment current tools? | Operator interviews and observation | Product direction |
| Who approves OEM/carrier profiles and resolves conflicts? | Operations and HSE | Real pilot gate |
| Does cosmetic damage require isolation at the site? | Approved HSE procedure | Concern categories |
| Which inspections require independent 12V dates/voltage? | Technical services | Observation model |
| Which VIN markets require check digits? | OEM/import specification | Validation policy |
| Which TOS supplies arrivals, positions and departure changes? | IT/data owner | Integration contract |
| What identity and tenant/OEM access boundaries apply? | IT/security/customer owners | Hosted release gate |
| How long must observations, reports and audit be retained? | Data/legal/contract owner | Retention and evidence |
| What real chargers and meter interfaces are accessible? | Equipment vendor/site engineering | OCPP/energy reconciliation |
| Who pays for software, onboarding and support? | Budget owner | Business model |
| What baseline and stop conditions define a successful pilot? | Sponsor/process owner | Pilot approval |

The current implementation deliberately leaves these as questions instead of inventing answers. Track resulting decisions in ADRs and anonymised validation summaries.
